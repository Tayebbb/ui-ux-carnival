## Tools and AI use

AI was an assistant, not the author. The eight-prompt pack at https://chatgpt.com/s/t_6aa430b886508191ac12f16790ebc536 was the **main workflow** (Copilot for Phases 1–3, Figma + Copilot with Figma MCP for Phases 4–6 and QA). Many additional prompts, audits and research passes were run during the sprint — judge simulations, Figma QA, accessibility checks, design-system research — and their outcomes are recorded in the decision log. Every decision was made by the entrant and is traceable there.
